# About Me

Hi, I'm Billy — an AI engineer based in London, working at the intersection of AI / data science and social good.

I'm currently an Applied AI Engineer at [IFS Nexus Black](https://www.ifs.com/nexus-black), IFS's industrial AI unit, building AI applications for industries like manufacturing, energy and aerospace.

## Experience

- **Applied AI Engineer**, [IFS Nexus Black](https://www.ifs.com/nexus-black) *(current)* — building industrial AI applications for asset- and service-intensive industries.
- **Senior Machine Learning Engineer**, [Currence.ai](https://currence.ai) (formerly Sightline Climate / CTVC) — helped investors, corporates and governments figure out where to put capital to actually move the needle on climate tech.
- **Founding AI Engineer**, [Cocomo](https://cocomo.ai/) — designed autonomous media buying and marketing optimisation driven by first-party data, as part of building out the SaaS platform.
- **Machine Learning Engineer**, [Trainline](https://www.trainline.com) — worked on the GenAI-powered Travel Assistant product.
- **Senior Data Scientist**, [EF Education First](https://www.ef.com) — built traditional ML applications across marketing, advertising, sales and operations.

## Communities & Side Projects

- **Co-Founder, [KindTech](https://kindte.ch/)** — making UK open data easy for charities, researchers and AI assistants to find and use, starting with a [CLI](https://github.com/KindTechUK/kindtech).
- **Claude Ambassador, London** — running community events in London, with a focus on helping non-profits put AI to work.
- **Co-Founder, [Reportable](https://reportable.uk/)** *(2020 – 2025)* — a volunteer group making it easier for East and South East Asian communities in the UK to report and get support around hate crime.
- **Crisis Modelling Lead, Europe Must Act** *(2020 – 2022)* — modelled COVID-19 spread in refugee camps and other low-resource settings, in collaboration with the Alan Turing Institute.
- **Co-Founder, AI for Good London** *(2019 – 2022)* — a community building hands-on AI projects for social impact.
- **Long-term volunteer, DataKind UK** — supporting charities with responsible data and AI use.
- **Summer Fellow**, Data Science for Social Good Foundation *(2020)*.
- **Talent**, UNLEASH — Innovation Lab for the UN SDGs, Shenzhen *(2019)*.
